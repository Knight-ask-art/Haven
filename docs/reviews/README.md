# Review Documentation Policy

A public review or PR comment must identify the inspected behavior and revision.
Resolution is demonstrated by a fix and relevant regression coverage, not by
changing the wording of an earlier review.

Raw diagnostics and internal review records remain private and preserve their
original context. Public navigation does not reproduce their filenames,
operational inventories or development-tool procedures.

See [Testing and Evidence](../engineering/testing-and-evidence.md) and
[Documentation Migration](../engineering/documentation-migration.md).
